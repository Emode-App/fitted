# emode Privacy Policy

Effective August 17, 2026

This policy explains how emode collects, uses, shares, retains, and deletes information when you use our fashion social, marketplace, messaging, and AI virtual try-on services.

## Information we collect

We collect account and profile information; posts, comments, messages, reports, wardrobe and product content; purchase, order, subscription, and seller-onboarding records; device push tokens and login-session details; support requests; and diagnostic events.

If you opt in to AI face processing, we process the face photo you select and clothing images to generate an outfit image. A face photo can be sensitive personal data. We do not use it to identify you, authenticate you, or train our own model.

## How information is used

We use information to operate accounts and social features, generate requested AI images, fulfill marketplace orders, verify subscriptions, prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our rules, provide support, send notifications you enable, and diagnose reliability or security problems.

## Service providers and sharing

We share only the information needed for a service with: Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and server functions; Google Gemini for requested AI generation and product-search prompts; RevenueCat and Apple or Google for subscriptions; Stripe for marketplace and styling-session payments and for seller and stylist onboarding; Expo for push delivery; and Sentry for diagnostics when enabled. Sellers receive the shipping and order details needed to fulfill purchases. A stylist you book receives the styling context you choose to share with them during your session, including a face photo if you send one. We may also disclose information when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

Your profile and content you intentionally publish are public, subject to blocking and moderation. Direct messages, stored face photos, and face-derived try-on outputs are access-controlled and are not public posts unless you separately choose to publish content.

## Choices and controls

You can edit your profile, control notification categories, block users, report content, revoke AI face consent, remove your saved face photo and generated try-ons, or permanently delete your account in the app. You can also submit an external deletion request below. Device settings control camera, photo, and notification permissions.

## Retention and deletion

Account content is retained while your account is active. In-app deletion removes the account and app content, including private face media and generated try-ons. We may retain limited transaction, fraud-prevention, tax, dispute, moderation, or legal records where required, then delete or de-identify them when no longer necessary. Processor systems may retain backups for a limited period under their own retention controls.

## Security, children, and changes

We use access controls, private storage, encrypted transport, server-side payment and entitlement verification, and monitoring. No system is perfectly secure. emode is not directed to children under 13; users must be old enough to consent under applicable law. We will post material policy changes here and update the effective date.
